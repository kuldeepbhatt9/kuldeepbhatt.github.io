ABOUT

Movement. Emotion.
Culture. Story.

Kuldeep Bhatt is a cinematographer, filmmaker and visual content creator based in Vadodara, India.

Working across automotive, branded content, entertainment, travel, live events and lifestyle, his work focuses on finding the emotion within movement and translating it into cinematic visual experiences.

His portfolio includes multiple editions of Royal Enfield Motoverse, automotive and motorsport films, artist and entertainment content, travel expeditions, sporting events, commercial interiors and social-first brand campaigns.

From concept and camera operation through editing and colour grading, Kuldeep approaches each project with one objective: create imagery that makes an audience feel something.
