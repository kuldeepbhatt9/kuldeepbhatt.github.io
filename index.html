ABOUT

Movement. Emotion.
Culture. Story.

I am a cinematographer, filmmaker and visual content creator based in Vadodara, India.

I work across automotive, branded content, entertainment, travel, live events and lifestyle. My focus is always on finding the emotion within movement and translating it into a cinematic visual experience.

My portfolio includes multiple editions of Royal Enfield Motoverse, automotive and motorsport films, artist and entertainment content, travel expeditions, sporting events, commercial interiors and digital brand campaigns.

From concept and camera operation through to editing and colour grading, I approach every project with one clear objective. I want to create imagery that makes an audience truly feel something.
